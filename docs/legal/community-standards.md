# MEN BEHIND WALL — Community Standards

MBW is an adults-only community built around dignity, consent, safety, authenticity, and lawful conduct.

## Required conduct

- Treat people with dignity.
- Respect consent and personal boundaries.
- Use authentic identity information.
- Protect private information.
- Follow local law.
- Report abuse, threats, exploitation, fraud, and unsafe conduct.

## Prohibited conduct

- Harassment, threats, stalking, hate, humiliation, or targeted abuse.
- Impersonation, scams, fraud, coercion, blackmail, or sextortion.
- Non-consensual intimate imagery or sexual content.
- Child sexual abuse material, grooming, exploitation, trafficking, or sexualization of minors.
- Promotion of terrorism, violent extremism, self-harm, illegal drugs, weapons trafficking, or criminal activity.
- Malware, credential theft, unauthorized automation, scraping, or service disruption.
- Sharing another person’s private information without permission.

## Enforcement

MBW may remove content, restrict features, suspend accounts, terminate access, preserve evidence, and notify authorities or safety organizations where required.
