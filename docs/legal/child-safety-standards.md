# MEN BEHIND WALL — Child Safety and CSAE Standards

MBW is restricted to adults aged 18 or older.

MBW maintains zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation, grooming, sextortion, trafficking, sexualization of minors, and child sexual abuse material.

## Detection and response

MBW may review reports, remove or restrict content, suspend or terminate accounts, preserve relevant evidence, and report confirmed violations to competent law-enforcement or child-safety authorities according to applicable law.

## User reporting

Users must report suspected child exploitation, grooming, trafficking, or child sexual abuse material immediately through the in-app reporting tools or the safety contact below.

## Cooperation

MBW cooperates with lawful investigations and may provide preserved evidence under valid legal process.
